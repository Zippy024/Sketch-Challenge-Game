# Mission thumbnails

`mission-thumbnails.webp` is an original generated photography-style 4 × 2 atlas,
created for the approved level selection design. Tiles show a sink, kitchen,
range hood, cabinets, refrigerator, cooktop, oven and pendant lights.
`MenuScreen.gd` selects tiles at runtime; the source atlas is not cropped or
combined with interface text. The Stage 1 thumbnail uses the uploaded reference
plan. These images illustrate missions and do not replace Stage 3 answer images.
